Privacy policy

DeutschEnglish

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Your music stays with you. plainblue Guitar works without an account, advertising or subscription. Practice, analysis and recording features run locally on your device. plainblue does not operate cloud storage for these features and does not automatically receive your recordings.

1. Provider and contact

This policy covers the Android app plainblue Guitar (org.plainblue.guitar) and this information website. The provider and contact for privacy enquiries is plainblue. Email: support@plainblue.org.

2. Data processed by the app

The app processes settings, language and notation preferences, instruments and tunings, chord sequences, routines, practice plans, diary entries, practice results, intonation measurements, game progress and high scores on your device. Recording projects may contain project and track names, video and audio files, and backing files you select. These data support the learning, practice and recording features you use.

There is no user account, advertising or analytics SDK, and no automatic transmission of your practice data or recordings to plainblue. The app does not perform automatic cloud synchronisation.

3. Microphone, camera and permissions

The microphone is used for tuning, intonation measurements, note and chord recognition, interactive exercises and recordings. Live recognition signals are analysed locally; this does not automatically create a saved audio recording. When you start a recording, audio and, where applicable, video are saved locally. The camera is used for video recordings and, where supported by the device, the optional flashlight metronome.

You choose files and backup folders through the system picker. Notifications support optional practice reminders and ongoing playback. The app can reschedule reminders after a device restart and keep the screen awake if you choose. You can change or revoke permissions in Android settings; dependent features may then be unavailable.

4. Optional backups, import, export and sharing

You can manually create a backup and select its destination folder. A full backup may include personal settings, practice data and audio or video recordings. If you select a cloud folder through the Android file picker, such as Google Drive, that service may transfer and store the files according to its settings and privacy policy. This transfer uses the service you selected, not a cloud service operated by plainblue.

When exporting or sharing, you choose the destination file, app or recipient. A gallery or selected storage provider may use its own automatic backups. Subsequent processing is governed by that provider's policies. Backup files are not encrypted with an additional app password; keep them secure.

5. Retention, security and deletion

App data are normally stored in the private app directory and protected by Android's security mechanisms. The app disables the regular Android app backup; backups you explicitly create remain available. Protection against every device loss or unauthorised access cannot be guaranteed.

Locally stored data remain available while you use them in the app or until you delete them. Clearing all app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app normally removes data in the private app directory. Removing individual entries or tracks does not necessarily delete every previously created copy. You must separately delete exported files, shared files and backups outside the app directory at their respective storage locations. plainblue does not hold a central copy of your local data and cannot remotely delete or restore them.

6. Email support

If you contact us, we process your email address, message and any attachments you voluntarily provide to handle your enquiry. Services used for email delivery and storage are involved. Please send only data necessary for your request. We retain correspondence for as long as necessary to handle it and, where applicable, fulfil statutory retention requirements or address legal claims; it is then deleted.

7. This website and external providers

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub receives connection data required to serve the page, particularly your IP address; GitHub logs IP addresses for security purposes. Processing outside the EU, particularly in the United States, is possible. For information about recipients, retention, privacy rights and the transfer mechanisms described by GitHub, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not embed analytics tools, advertising, external fonts, videos or contact forms on this site, and we do not set cookies ourselves. Linked external pages are accessed only when you open their links. App downloads, updates and use of an app store are additionally subject to that store provider's privacy policy, such as the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Purposes and legal bases

Where plainblue processes personal data and the GDPR applies, providing requested services and handling related enquiries is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For other support enquiries and secure delivery of this information website, we rely on our legitimate interests in communication and reliable information (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where consent is required and obtained, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR applies; statutory retention obligations are based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. An operating system permission does not automatically constitute consent under data protection law.

9. Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent with effect for the future. You may lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority, particularly where you habitually reside. Send enquiries to support@plainblue.org. We cannot access data held solely on your device.

Practice assessments and high scores support your learning. They are not automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.

10. Changes

If features or data processing change, we will update this policy and the date above.
